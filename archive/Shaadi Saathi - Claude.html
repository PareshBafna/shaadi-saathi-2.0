Features, explained

All 14 features, most-used first

Each feature below says what it does, how to use it in a few taps, who it is for and why it matters. Tap any feature to open it.

01Guest list and RSVP

Every guest in one list with their side, number of people, invite version and reply status.

How to use it

  1. Tap Add guest. Enter name, side, people and invite version.
  2. Tap the status pill to switch between Attending, Pending and Declined.
  3. Use search and the filter chips to find who has not replied.
  4. Import a spreadsheet, or export the list as CSV at any time.

Best for

Families and planners

Why it matters

You always know the exact headcount and who still needs a reminder.

02WhatsApp invites and reminders

Each guest gets their invite in a WhatsApp thread. Reminders go only to guests who have not replied.

How to use it

  1. Pick the guest or the whole list.
  2. Send the invite. It opens with the guest’s own events.
  3. Tap Remind pending guests to follow up in one step.

Best for

Everyone

Why it matters

Invites arrive where guests already read messages. No app to install.

03Invite versions

Choose which events each guest is invited to: Version A (Reception only), B (Sangeet and Reception) or C (all four events).

How to use it

  1. Set the version when you add a guest, or change it later.
  2. Their invite and reminders show only those events.
  3. Check the count on the Events page to see who is invited to what.

Best for

Families with many functions

Why it matters

Close family gets every event, colleagues get only the reception. No wrong invites.

04Rooms and split stays

A room board with bed capacity. Assign each attending guest or family member to a room.

How to use it

  1. Choose the guest and the room, then tap Assign.
  2. A full room is blocked, so nobody is double-booked.
  3. Tap Remove to move someone to another room.

Best for

Families with outstation guests

Why it matters

One family can be split across two rooms, and every bed is accounted for.

05Travel and pickup

Record each guest’s flight or train, number, PNR, arrival time and whether pickup is arranged.

How to use it

  1. Add an arrival: choose the guest, flight or train, number and time.
  2. Set pickup to Pending or Assigned.
  3. Guests can also add their own travel details on their pass.

Best for

Families with guests flying or taking trains

Why it matters

Airport and station pickups are planned from one list, not from calls.

06Budget ledger

A running total of the budget, what is paid and what is still due.

How to use it

  1. Each vendor or cost line shows its total and amount paid.
  2. Tap Mark fully paid when a bill is settled.
  3. Totals at the top update straight away.

Best for

Families and planners

Why it matters

You see the balance before the next payment is due.

07Vendors

A card for each service: venue, catering, decoration, DJ and sound, anchoring, photography.

How to use it

  1. Tap Add vendor. Choose the category, enter the name and the total.
  2. Track paid and due amounts on each card.
  3. Mark a vendor paid when the final amount is cleared.

Best for

Planners and hosts

Why it matters

Every vendor contract and payment is in one place.

08Family tasks

A to-do board with an owner and a status for each task.

How to use it

  1. Tap Add task. Write the task and who will do it.
  2. Tap the status to move it: Not Started, In Progress, Done.
  3. Check the board in the family group before each function.

Best for

Families dividing the work

Why it matters

Nothing falls between cousins and uncles. Everyone sees who owns what.

09Wedding events schedule

Each ceremony with its date, time and venue, such as Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet and Reception.

How to use it

  1. Add each event with date, time and venue.
  2. The invite versions pick from this list.
  3. Guests see the schedule for their own events.

Best for

Everyone

Why it matters

Guests know when and where to be, for every function they are invited to.

10Guest pass

A private page for each guest: their events, room, travel and reply.

How to use it

  1. Guest opens their pass from the invite link.
  2. They see their events, room and pickup time.
  3. They can reply and add their own travel details.

Best for

Guests

Why it matters

Guests find their own details without calling the family.

11Digital guestbook

A wall where guests leave blessings and wishes for the couple.

How to use it

  1. Guest types name and blessing, then posts.
  2. The family reads every message on the wedding page.

Best for

Guests and couples

Why it matters

A keepsake that grows through the wedding.

12Photo gallery

A shared photo wall where guests and family add pictures with a caption.

How to use it

  1. Tap Add photo, choose a picture, add a caption.
  2. The photo appears on the wall for everyone to see.

Best for

Guests and families

Why it matters

Every guest’s photos are gathered in one album.

13Host and creator access

Families hosting see their own weddings. Planners see all weddings they run.

How to use it

  1. Host: switch between the bride’s or groom’s family hosting accounts.
  2. Creator: see every wedding, its replies and totals in one screen.

Best for

Planners and both families

Why it matters

Each person sees only the weddings and details they are responsible for.

14Languages

Salutations and invite text in English, Hindi, Gujarati and Marwari.

How to use it

  1. Choose the invite language when you send.
  2. Elders read the invite in the language they know best.

Best for

Families with elder guests

Why it matters

Older relatives understand the invite without help.

FAQ

Common questions

Which number are invitations sent from?

From our verified WhatsApp Business number. You do not need to register a number or use your personal SIM.

How do invite versions work?

You choose which events each guest is invited to. That guest's invite and reminders show only those events.

One family gets one invite, but 3 members come and need 2 rooms?

Add each attending member and assign rooms per person. The family sees one list with their own room and travel details.

How are Creator, Host and Guest access separated?

Creators see all weddings. Hosts manage their own weddings. Guests see only their own pass, room and travel.
