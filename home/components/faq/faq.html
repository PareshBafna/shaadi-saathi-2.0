01 How do WhatsApp message credits and the ₹1/message top-up work?
WhatsApp Credits +
Silver (₹9,999) includes 2,000 credits, Royal Gold (₹14,999) includes 4,000 credits, and Imperial (₹19,999) includes 6,000 credits. 1 credit equals 1 official green-tick WhatsApp message dispatched to a guest (such as an invitation, RSVP reminder, room key alert, or travel pickup update). If you need extra messages, you can top up anytime at a flat ₹1 per message.
02 How do Invite Variants (A, B & C) hide uninvited events from guests?
Invite Privacy +
In Indian weddings, office colleagues may only be invited to the Reception, while close cousins attend Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, and Shaadi. When you assign a family to Variant A (1 Event), Variant B (2 Events), or Variant C (All Events), their personalized WhatsApp link and 3D Web Invite dynamically display only their assigned ceremonies—so no guest ever feels left out.
03 Which plans include the 3D Wax-Seal Web Invite and Custom WhatsApp Templates?
Plans & Upgrades +
The 3D Interactive Wax-Seal Web Invitation (with falling petals, Shehnai audio, and Google Maps pins) is included in both the ₹14,999 Royal Gold and ₹19,999 Imperial plans, or can be purchased standalone / added to the ₹9,999 Silver plan for ₹3,999. Custom Meta-approved WhatsApp Message Templates are included in the ₹19,999 Imperial plan or available as a ₹2,499 add-on.
04 Can we allot different hotel rooms to members of the same family invitation?
Room Allotments +
Yes! A single family invitation often has 4 or 5 attending members who need to split across two rooms or even different properties. Inside Stay Accommodation, you can mark whether a family Needs Stay or is Local (No Room), and allot specific hotels and room numbers per individual family member.
05 How do Multiple Family Host Profiles work for Bride side, Groom side & Elders?
Family Hosts +
Traditional invitations carry the names of grandparents, Bride’s parents, or Groom’s parents depending on who is inviting the guest. You can create unlimited individual Host Profiles so Bride-side guests receive invitations with the Bride’s parents' salutation, while Groom-side guests see the Groom’s family elders.
06 What if some attending guests are local and do not need hotel rooms?
Local Guests +
Inside the Guest list, toggle whether an attending family Needs Stay or is Local (No Room). Only guests marked Needs Stay appear in your Stay Accomodation queue, so local families never clutter room allotments.
07 Can we buy a standalone service (like WhatsApp Broadcast or 3D Web Invite) without a full plan?
Standalone Services +
Absolutely. Every item in our Standalone Modules & Add-Ons section can be purchased independently without a full wedding suite, or bolted onto an existing Silver, Royal Gold, or Imperial plan whenever your family needs it.
08 How are Creator, Family Host, Logistics Coordinator, and Guest permissions separated?
Role Security +
When you open Planner OS, you see all weddings organized by your role in each celebration: Creator Super Admin, Family Host Admin, Logistics Coordinator, or Invited Guest Pass. Each role opens only the services it is allowed to use.
