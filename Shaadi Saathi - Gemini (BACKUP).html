FAQ

Common questions answered clearly

How do WhatsApp message credits and the ₹1/message top-up work?

Silver (₹9,999) includes 2,000 credits, Royal Gold (₹14,999) includes 4,000 credits, and Imperial (₹19,999) includes 6,000 credits. 1 credit equals 1 WhatsApp message sent to a guest. If you need additional messages, you can top up anytime at a flat ₹1 per message.

Which plans include the 3D Web Invite and Custom WhatsApp Templates?

The 3D Interactive Wax-Seal Web Invitation is included in both the ₹14,999 (Royal Gold) and ₹19,999 (Imperial) plans, or can be added to the ₹9,999 plan for ₹3,999 as a standalone add-on. Custom WhatsApp Message Templates are exclusive to the ₹19,999 Imperial plan (or available as an add-on).

Are Flight Arrangements and Train Arrangements managed separately?

Yes. Inside the workspace, Flight Arrangements and Train Arrangements have dedicated tables so airport pickup teams and railway station hospitality desks can operate independently without clutter.

How are Creator, Host, and Guest permissions separated?

The Creator dashboard displays all weddings managed on the platform. The Host portal displays only weddings hosted by that family plus Guest Passes for weddings they are invited to. The Guest Pass displays only that specific family's invited events, room allotments, and linked travel PNR.
